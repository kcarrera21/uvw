## Love It Loud Rock & Roll Wedding Chapel

- Slug: `love-it-loud-rock-roll-chapel`. One record. KISS is a search alias, not a second venue.
- Ceremony page: https://rocknrollchapel.com/ceremony-packages/
- Reception page: https://rocknrollchapel.com/receptions/
- Address on both pages: Rio All-Suite Hotel & Casino, Masquerade Village, casino level, 3700 W Flamingo Rd, Las Vegas, NV 89103.
- `priceFrom` 299 is Hotter Than Hell only. The page says a traditional minister, no substitutions, a ceremony host, and up to 30 guests. An 18% service fee is added to the final balance. The fee is in the label, not added into 299, because the page does not print the fee-included total.
- Whole Lotta Love is $649 before the same 18% fee, with a choice of character minister. Higher packages are $979 and $1,179. Extra characters are $75, $150, or $275. Those are not the starting price.
- Character ministers are impersonators. The page says so by calling them character ministers.
- `guestMax` is null. Thirty guests is the ceremony-package cap, not a published building maximum. Reception packages start at a minimum of 20 and the page also says groups of all sizes, so a single maximum would be a guess.
- Reception is separate. Premier is $799 plus tax and an 18% service charge, minimum 20, with glow minigolf and a taco bar. Black Diamond is $859 on the same terms. That is not included in $299.
- Not the Punk Rock Museum. Not the Sci-Fi Wedding Chapel at Horseshoe.
