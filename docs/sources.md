- Marriage license requirements: https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/elected_officials/county_clerk/marriage-license-requirements
- Fees ($102 license; $20 certified marriage certificate; card fee 2% + $1.25): https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/elected_officials/county_clerk/fees
- Locations & hours (8 a.m.–midnight daily, 201 E. Clark Ave.): https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/elected_officials/county_clerk/location-and-hours
- Proof of marriage / certified copies: https://www.clarkcountynv.gov/government/elected_officials/county_clerk/what-is-proof-of-marriage
