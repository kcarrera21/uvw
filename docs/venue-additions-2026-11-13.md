## Real Bodies at Horseshoe (Catacombs Weddings)

- Slug: `real-bodies-horseshoe-catacombs`. Category `quirky-pop-culture`.
- Source: https://www.caesars.com/horseshoe-las-vegas/things-to-do/weddings
- `priceFrom` 1649 is the Til Death Do Us Part package, "starting at $1,649 plus tax". `guestMax` 12 is that package's cap.
- Inclusions copied as listed: planner and day-of coordinator, 6-rose bouquet, boutonniere, rose petals, 12 luminaries, preselected playlist, champagne for the couple, 30 minutes of photography, (3) 4x6, (2) 5x7, (1) 8x10. Extra exhibit tickets $25 per guest.
- Unknown and left unknown: officiant fee, gratuity, address line, phone.
- Photo: Unsplash ossuary in Switzerland (Malcolm Lightbody, photo 4hhEvTB45vg). Marked `illustrative`. The venue uses faux bones; this photo is not the venue.
