## Contact, before and after

Before: "Something on the site is wrong." "You want a date or a price." "We can't tell you if a Saturday is free."

After: "Spotted something we should update?" and "Checking prices or available dates? For current packages and availability, head to the venue's official website from its listing. You'll hear directly from the people who can help you book." The opening invites a question or a correction. The email is still hello@uniquevegasweddings.com. No reply-time promise was added.

## For venues

Before: "List a place couples can't book anywhere else."

After: "Help couples find your venue." The homepage band no longer uses that exclusivity line. Prices, plan bullets, and form fields are unchanged.

## Other wording changes

- Shark Reef, Caesars Palace, Battlefield Vegas, Flamingo gardens, and Hofbräuhaus: dropped "few venues anywhere," "one of the few," "nobody else in town," "best-kept secrets," and "nobody will forget." The tanks, guest counts, Humvee, tank, gardens, and beer halls stay.
- Graceland and Viva Las Vegas: "where it started" and "longer than anyone" now match the listings, which say the chapels call or bill themselves the first.
- Oasis at Death Valley: the elopement line now says 2 to 8 guests and a dinner for two, matching the facts on that listing. It had said the package was built for two.
- Westin Lake Las Vegas: the yacht line now says up to 100, which is the published yacht capacity. It had said "take everyone."
- Sanders Family Winery: "an hour" is now "about 45 minutes," matching the drive line on that listing.
- Courthouse guide: one witness plus eight guests is nine other people, not ten.
- License FAQ: the "no residency requirement" answer now keeps the 17-year-old court-order caveat already in the guide body.
- Weekend guide: dropped "talking about it at your tenth anniversary" and named the useful details (where people stay, how they get there, a note with the times).
- Cannabis guide heading "Consumption lounges open now" is now "Consumption lounges, as of October 2025," which is the date of the FOX5 report in the next sentence.
- Gothic guide: dropped "longer than anyone" and "the deepest in town." The address and the year-round line stay.
- Tunnel of Love post: dropped "a gimmick, sure" after the chapel's accessibility origin.
- Guest-logistics post: the 3:30 p.m. last ceremony is now limited to the Visitor Center Amphitheater, as the Easter and Cinco de Mayo posts already say.
- Accessibility post answer: "built for wheeled devices" now uses the museum's own wording from the same article ("easy to navigate with powered and unpowered wheeled devices").
- Shared headings "Frequently asked questions" and "Why you'll love it" remain "Questions" and "What it's like" from the earlier commit. Checklist button remains "Send the checklist."

## Left on purpose

The other 94 venue descriptions were already specific (a pink Cadillac, a sauce-packet bouquet, a permit lead time, a music curfew). "We love" stayed where it pointed at a real package detail. Guide and post bodies that were already plain were not rewritten for rhythm. "We're glad you're thinking about it" on the elopement guide was kept. Cutting it would have made the page colder.

Disclosure, privacy, terms, and photo credits were read and not edited. The privacy and terms pages still say they are templates to review before launch. That wording is stale on a live site, and it was left so the legal meaning and the caution stay intact. The photo-credit "within a week" note was left.

Budget planner calculations, wedding-finder question ids, and form actions were not touched.

## Reported, not "fixed"

These disagree inside the site. No new number was chosen.

- Red Rock commercial permit minimum is $130 in the spring post and $345 in the Easter and Cinco de Mayo posts.
- The courthouse guide says the courthouse is cheaper than the chapels. Little Neon's $69 Sign & Go is lower, and that guide already refuses to fold it into an all-in total.
- Taco Bell's package line says up to 25 guests. A tip on the same listing says other parts of the site mention 15. The tip already tells the reader to confirm.
- Oasis facts say the dinner with paired wines is for two, while the package takes 2 to 8 guests. The description now says both. It does not invent a headcount for the dinner.
- Machine Guns Vegas lists more than one ceremony price and more than one place the ceremony starts. Both are already in the facts.
- The Grove, Renowned Events, Sandy Valley Ranch, and Palms chapel have internal price or capacity clashes already named in their tips or facts.

## What this pass is not

It is not a claim that every sentence was rewritten. It is a claim that the bodies above were read and that the edits are the ones a second draft actually earned.
